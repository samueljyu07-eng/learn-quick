Photosynthesis is how plants make food.

Plants take in light, water, and carbon dioxide. They use light to turn water and carbon dioxide into sugar and oxygen. The sugar stores energy the plant can use later.

Chlorophyll is the green pigment that captures light. It sits inside chloroplasts, which are parts of the plant cell.

A common mix-up: plants also use oxygen when they break sugar down for energy. Photosynthesis produces oxygen. It does not mean plants never use oxygen.
